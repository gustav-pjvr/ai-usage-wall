## Constraints the review must respect
- The wall sits behind other windows and is `focusable: false`; typing borrows focus (notes bar, Ask, comments). Renderer has no network (`connect-src 'none'`); all fetches are in main.
- MEASURED / DERIVED badges, never a guessed number; missing data hides a panel rather than painting 0.
- Untrusted text (mail, monday, model output) is always `textContent`; no HTML.
- Sizes 1280x800 to 2560x1440 must fit with no overflow (render-fit enforces this).
- No em or en dashes in anything written.
